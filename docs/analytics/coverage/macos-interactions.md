### Live composer privacy revocation

Captured composer scopes are now remembered weakly. State updates classify current active reply/forward/edit sources before deferred-action invalidation and observer notification. When those sources become private or unresolved, the remembered scopes are explicitly excluded. Contexts derived from the same scope become excluded too. The existing policy revocation path discards recorder state and invalidates the pending transport context; already dispatched events cannot be recalled.

Presentation leases observe explicit privacy exclusion independently of ordinary generation invalidation. An already-open payment/picker lifetime therefore becomes excluded immediately, including when its scope was previously revoked for another reason. Visible screen contexts are reclassified on policy reset, and their exclusions survive until each screen closes. Other ordinary scopes lose their old generation as before, but are not permanently labelled private. New independent ordinary contexts can be captured after private descendants close.

The composer registry is atomic and carries a privacy revision. A capture that overlaps a private update is excluded if its registration revision is stale. Scope callbacks run outside the registry lock. The registry does not retain scopes, controllers or finished actions. This guards the captured composer action tree; producers or surfaces that bypass composer capture still require their own audit.
