They cover calendar-based day/week/month movement, Gregorian limits, leap/year/DST behavior, focus versus selection, localization formatting and real accessibility role/value/enabled/focused/press/weak-parent contracts.

Regression projections execute extracted baseline step/Return/done and current Return/Escape/done bodies.
