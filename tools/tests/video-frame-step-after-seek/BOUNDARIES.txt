The harness extracts the actual controller rewind closure, frame-step and playback methods, and the regular/PiP timeline and15-second control callbacks from SVideoView. It checks seeking while paused, forward/backward steps, repeated steps without intermediate status delivery, boundary clamping, weak lifetime, normal playing-scrub pause/resume and disposal of the obsolete preview subscription.

Player status, player seeks and preview subscriptions use explicit synchronous fixtures. This is a control-flow regression, not a decoder, HLS streaming, SignalKit queue, network, window or Telegram-account test. Disposal prevents later emission in the fixture; real SignalKit delivery behavior and in-flight media generation are not simulated. Manual visual acceptance remains separate.

The production change invalidates the old frame-step accumulator and detaches/hides its preview when the existing non-frame rewind callback runs. It retains the existing30fps step interval and does not change frame-accurate decoding policy.
